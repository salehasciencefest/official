Registration Deadline Shortened

August 27, 2026

Due to an overwhelming number of student applications and submissions, the original deadline for Saleha Science Fest has been shortened to ensure prompt and fair evaluation for all participants. ​

New Deadline for Offline Registration: 1st September, 2026 -Wednesday

New Deadline for Online Registration: 2nd September, 2026 -Thursday

Submissions received after the revised deadline will not be considered. If you have already submitted your materials, no further action is required. We strongly encourage all remaining candidates to complete their submissions as early as possible.

We appreciate the tremendous interest and enthusiasm from the student community. For any urgent inquiries, please contact salehasciencefest@gmail.com.
