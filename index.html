​Forget the textbooks. Science was never meant to be memorized- it was built to be tested. ​The Sirajganj Town Science Fest isn't just another competition. It is an arena created by students, for students, to bring the ultimate academic stage right here to our town. Whether you're here to solve tough logic puzzles, prove your mastery in physics and chemistry or step up to the mic, this is your platform to show everyone what you're capable of. ​Bring your sharpest thinking, represent your school, and take your spot among the brightest young minds in the region.
