​How We Are Planning This Year’s Fest

August 13, 2026

This initiative was created with a clear purpose: to give students in our town access to the same high-caliber academic opportunities and competitive platforms found in major cities. Organized entirely by students for students, every detail of this fest has been designed to foster genuine curiosity and academic excellence. ​

✔Rigorous Academic Standards

To ensure a fair and meaningful competition, our examination questions are curated by top-performing senior students who possess a deep understanding of the national curriculum.

✔Structured Sectors:

Primary Sector: Classes 3–5

Junior Sector: Classes 6–8

Secondary Sector: Classes 9–10

✔​Standardized Testing:

A focused, 50-mark Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) exam with a 1-hour time limit, strictly evaluated with standard negative marking (0.25 marks deducted per incorrect answer) to encourage precision and critical thinking.

✔Beyond the Written Test:

​We believe a science festival should be a celebration of learning, not just an examination. Following the test and a dedicated refreshment break, the afternoon transitions into an interactive event featuring:

→​Distinguished Guest Speakers: Insights and encouragement from experienced science educators and guest mentors.

→​School & Interactive Stalls: Dedicated spaces for participating schools to showcase scientific talent and creative ideas.

→​​Live Audience Quiz: An engaging, fast-paced stage quiz offering instant recognition and prizes for quick thinkers.

✔Merit & Recognition:

​A total of 31 awards will be presented during our closing ceremony- including prizes for top audience quiz participants and official honours for the top 7 rankers across each of the three competition sectors.
