About the fest

A festival built by students for students interested in science

Saleha Science Fest is a student-run science festival that aims to provide a platform for students to explore and showcase their scientific talents.

The idea for our fest came from seeing that science festivals are already being organized in different cities, but our area didn't have anything like this yet. So, we thought, why not start something new here? As the first science fest of its kind in our area, we wanted to give students an opportunity to experience something different beyond textbooks. We created a platform where everyone can share their ideas, show their creativity, and have fun while learning through different activities, projects, and experiments. Since it's the first event like this here, we hope everyone will participate and make it a memorable experience.

Our goals

Make students interested in science

Provide a platform for students to showcase their talents

Encourage collaboration and learning
